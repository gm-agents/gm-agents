## Hi, I'm Girish

I am a product leader who builds mobile products in financial services. Outside the day job I work on AI-native product management, which we define as "the discipline of turning a probabilistic system into a product that reliably solves a real problem."

I have worked across product, engineering, design, research and finance, and I did graduate research in human factors at Bentley University. That background is why I care as much about the person using an AI product as about the model inside it.

### What I am building

- **[AI-Native Product Management](https://ainativeproductmanager.com)** is a free site I write with Shivali Manghani. It holds our product frameworks and The Builder's Stack, a hands-on course that starts with how software works and ends with running an AI organization. It has no paywall, no account and no ads.
- **[technicalenough.ai](https://technicalenough.ai)** is its sister site, which teaches the technology fundamentals to people who have never shipped software.
- **[FuelTheFam](https://fuelthefam.com)** is a family nutrition app we built with AI. We wrote it up as a [case study](https://ainativeproductmanager.com/case-study/fuelthefam) of the course applied to a real product.

### What is in this account

- **[vibe-resources](https://github.com/gm-agents/vibe-resources)** is where I keep reusable prompts and rules for working with AI tools. It includes the writing rules we hold our own content to, and a budget routine for tasks where one coding agent starts many others.
- **[gift-of-time](https://github.com/gm-agents/gift-of-time)** is a set of agent skills for getting hours back, such as a meeting audit and a plan for household chores. Each skill was tested against the same model working without it.

### How I work

I build with AI coding agents, mostly Claude Code. When the agent gets a draft wrong, I write the correction into a rules file so the next draft starts from it. I hold to the principle we publish on the site: use AI for speed, and keep people in charge of what "good" means.
