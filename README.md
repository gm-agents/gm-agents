## Hi, I am Girish

I am a VP of Product at Fidelity Investments. I have worked across product, engineering, design, research and finance, and I did graduate research in human factors at Bentley University. That background is why I care as much about the human using an AI product as about the model inside it. Outside my day job I work on developing ideas, perspectives and tactics on AI-native product management. I consider myself completely AI pilled.

### What I am building (private repositories)

- **[A Comprehensive Training and Thought Leadership on AI-Native Product Management](https://ainativeproductmanager.com)** is a free resource that I have created. It holds my product frameworks and The Builder's Stack, a hands-on course that starts with how software works and ends with running an AI organization. It has no paywall, no account and no ads.
- **[technicalenough.ai](https://technicalenough.ai)** is its sister site, which teaches the technology fundamentals to people who have never shipped software.
- **[FuelTheFam](https://fuelthefam.com)** is a family nutrition app we built with AI. We wrote it up as a [case study](https://ainativeproductmanager.com/case-study/fuelthefam) of the course applied to a real product.

### What else is in this account (public repositories)

- **[vibe-resources](https://github.com/gm-agents/vibe-resources)** is where I keep reusable prompts and rules for working with AI tools. It includes the writing rules I hold my own content to, and a budget routine for tasks where one coding agent starts many others.
- **[gift-of-time](https://github.com/gm-agents/gift-of-time)** is a set of agent skills for getting time back, such as a meeting audit and a plan for household chores. Each skill was tested against the same model working without it.

### How I work

I build with AI coding agents, mostly Claude Code. I hold to the principle we published on our site: use AI for speed, and keep people in charge of what "good" means.
